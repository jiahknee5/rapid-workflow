brainstorm canvas — what you provide vs. what you review · notes persist locally
naming (fork #1):

Four gates are your only touchpoints. Everything else, you provide once or watch continuously.

This board separates the three roles the current architecture diagram blurs together: documents you provide / sign, documents you review at a gate, and live surfaces you only watch. The goal is to decide which of these belong in the architecture/workflow diagram — and how the doc nav should reconcile to them.

The hypothesis we're testing: "the highest-level documents = the ones outlined in the documentation (the nav)." Short answer — the nav is missing half your real review documents and promotes several things you never approve. The operator-decision set is the four gate bundles, not the nav order.
