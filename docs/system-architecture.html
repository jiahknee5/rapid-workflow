RAPID — System Architecture

The complete map of the RAPID build system: a layered pipeline that takes a product idea through vision → panels → derived spec → a bounded agent swarm → a gap loop, and out the other side as a deployed, documented app with its own Atlas. Every feature and capability below gets its own diagram.

Reading order is top-to-bottom = idea-to-ship. Boxes are color-coded by kind (see Legend). The four newest capabilities — A per-phase cost, B inputs ledger, C verified worktrees, E design-in-plan — are drawn in purple.

System at a glance

One product idea in, one shipped + documented app out. Humans are consulted at four gates, all front-loaded before the build; after Gate 2 the build runs to completion on its own.
